2. Information we may collect

  • Registration and contact details: your name, email address, telephone number, business name or role, location and information you choose to provide when registering or asking a question.
  • Payment and transaction information: ticket selection, payment status, amount and confirmation references. Card or bank credentials should be handled by the payment provider, not sent to us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Training and community information: attendance, questions, workbook responses, messages and feedback you choose to share during the Training or optional WhatsApp community.
  • Technical information: limited device, browser, IP or log information generated by the website, registration service or virtual training platform. We will update this notice and request consent where required before using non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies.

3. Why we use it

We use personal information to process your registration and payment; provide joining details, materials, replay access and support; run and improve the Training; communicate important service changes; protect participants and our systems; comply with legal obligations; and, where you have opted in, send relevant Events by AD updates.

We rely on the legal basis that fits the purpose, including taking steps at your request or performing our contract with you, consent, a legal obligation, and our legitimate interests in operating a safe and effective training business. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing already carried out before withdrawal.

4. When we share information

We share only what is necessary with service providers who help us operate the Training, such as payment processors, registration and email providers, virtual-event platforms, cloud storage or support providers. We may also share information with instructors or event delivery partners where needed to run the Training, with WhatsApp if you voluntarily join the community, or with authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal information. Our providers must handle information only for authorised purposes and with appropriate safeguards.

5. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside Nigeria. Where this happens, we will use the safeguards and transfer mechanism required by applicable data-protection law, taking account of the country and service involved.

6. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including delivering the Training, managing enquiries and meeting accounting, tax, dispute-resolution or other legal requirements. The exact period depends on the record and purpose. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or de-identify it in line with our retention practices.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or erase personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; receive a portable copy of information you provided; withdraw consent; and seek review of a solely automated decision that significantly affects you. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

To make a request, email [[LEGAL CONTACT EMAIL]] with enough information for us to verify your identity and understand your request. We may ask for further information where reasonably necessary to protect your data.

8. Security

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No online service can be completely secure, so please keep your access links and account details private and tell us promptly if you believe they have been compromised.

9. Children

The Training is designed for event professionals and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without the appropriate authority, please contact us so that we can review and address it.

10. Updates to this notice

We may update this notice to reflect changes in our practices, service providers or legal obligations. The revised version will be posted here with an updated date. Where a change is material, we will take appropriate steps to notify registered participants.
