What is The Event Business Blueprint?

The Event Business Blueprint is a live online business training for event planners and event professionals in Nigeria and across Africa. Over two practical evenings, Events by AD helps participants build better pricing, systems, client experience, vendor collaboration, and a clearer plan for growing an event business.
